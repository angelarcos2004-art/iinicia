## Brand & Style

The design system projects technological precision, scientific inquiry, and dynamic academic evolution. Grounded in higher education, research, and applied sciences, the interface balances institutional credibility with the kinetic vibrancy of modern digital pedagogy.

### Visual Aesthetic: Tech-Forward Institutional Modernism
- **Clarity and Precision:** Generous white space, purposeful structural alignment, and crisp geometric contrast reflect laboratory-grade precision.
- **Electric Scientific Resonance:** Vibrancy is channeled through the signature electric cyan and deep indigo-cobalt spectrum of the mark, signaling innovation, connectivity, and applied intelligence.
- **Tactile Trust & Layering:** Restrained elevations, fine low-contrast borders, and luminous cyan-tinted atmospheric shadows create an interface engineered for clarity and cognitive endurance.

## Colors

The color system directly derives from the electric cyan mark and its deep structural counterweights, rigorously structured to guarantee WCAG 2.2 AAA/AA compliance across light surfaces.

### Functional Roles & Hierarchies
- **Primary (`#009EE2`):** The signature electric cyan extracted from the emblem. Applied to key focal highlights, interactive indicators, primary progress meters, active pagination nodes, and graphic accents. When text is placed on this cyan surface, high-contrast dark tones (`#002855` or `#0F172A`) are enforced.
- **Secondary (`#005A94`):** The deep accessible ocean cyan-blue. Utilized for interactive text links, primary actionable buttons on light grounds, sub-navigation tabs, and outlined component boundaries. Provides a robust 5.8:1 contrast ratio against white.
- **Tertiary / Institutional Ground (`#002855`):** Midnight navy tone from the deeper brand spectrum. Applied to global headers, master navigation shells, hero display headlines, and high-impact institutional cards, achieving an 11:1+ AAA contrast ratio on light canvases.
- **Surfaces & Neutrals:**
  - `surface-canvas` (`#FFFFFF`): Pure white primary background for maximum luminosity.
  - `surface-subtle` (`#F8FAFC`): Crisp off-white for alternating rows and soft section breaks.
  - `surface-container` (`#F0F6FA`): Cyan-tinted cool white for component backdrops and input containers.
  - `text-primary` (`#0F172A`): Deep slate for body copy, data metrics, and high-legibility labels.
  - `text-secondary` (`#475569`): Secondary guidance copy and metadata.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy is unified across all breakpoints with **Plus Jakarta Sans**, aligning structural headlines and reading body text under a clean, contemporary geometric rhythm.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set with tightened letter-spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` and `800`) to evoke architectural presence and forward-looking momentum.
- **Body & Data Prose:** Rendered with standard letter spacing and balanced line heights (1.6 to 1.625) to preserve clarity in dense educational syllabi and laboratory research portals.
- **Tabular Figures:** Numbers in academic credit tables, timestamps, and statistics must use standard tabular digits (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`).

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive 12-column fluid grid system governs spatial balance, organizing content into predictable, easily scannable vertical blocks.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout, max-width `1360px`, `margin-desktop` (3rem), and `gutter-desktop` (2rem). Supports multi-pane scientific dashboards and 3-to-4 column academic card decks.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `margin` (1.5rem), and `gutter` (1.5rem). Elements regroup into 2-column modules.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile` (1.25rem), and `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Grid elements reflow into vertical full-width stacks.

### Spacing Scale
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Icon-label gaps, chip insets, and inner badge padding.
- `space-md` (16px): Input field internal padding, stack gaps, and list item spacing.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card interior paddings and component cluster separation.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks and header-to-body transitions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through translucent cyan-tinted atmospheric ambient shadows and delicate low-contrast borders, maintaining an open, luminous appearance.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** Surfaces rest flush on `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC` accompanied by a `1px solid #E2E8F0` hairline border. Ideal for inactive fields, static content groupings, and academic tables.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(0, 90, 148, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 158, 226, 0.06);`
  - Framed by a subtle `#E2E8F0` border. Applied to degree program cards, researcher profiles, and metric displays.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Contexts):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(0, 90, 148, 0.06), 0 12px 24px -4px rgba(0, 158, 226, 0.12);`
  - Triggered during cursor hover states, flyout menus, and active card selections.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -6px rgba(0, 40, 85, 0.16), 0 24px 48px -8px rgba(0, 158, 226, 0.10);`
  - Applied to application modals, sticky header navigation bars, and slide-in drawers.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy leverages geometric continuity inspired by the circular, connected nodes of the logo mark.

### Corner Radius System
- **Subtle (`rounded-sm` - 4px):** Checkboxes, nested badges, and precise technical UI tags.
- **Core / Standard (`rounded-md` - 8px / 0.5rem):** Form text inputs, dropdown menus, and standard button elements.
- **Containers (`rounded-lg` - 12px to 16px):** Course modules, feature highlight containers, and modal dialogs.
- **Capsule (`rounded-full`):** Filter pills, status chips, avatar silhouettes, and circular icon nodes reflecting the mark's geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion / CTA):** Background `#005A94`, text `#FFFFFF`, font-weight `600`, border-radius `8px` (`rounded-md`), height `48px`, horizontal padding `space-lg`. Hover: `#004777` with Level 2 shadow.
- **Accent Action (Highlight / Active State):** Background `#009EE2`, text `#002855`, font-weight `700`. Hover: `#008bc7`.
- **Secondary (Outline):** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #005A94`, text `#005A94`, font-weight `600`. Hover: background `#F0F6FA`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#005A94`. Hover: background `#E0F2FE`.

### Academic Cards
- Constructed with a `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px solid #E2E8F0` outline, and `16px` radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Padding: `24px` (`space-lg`).
- Hover Behavior: Elevates smoothly to Level 2 with a dynamic `border-color: #009EE2` transition.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Height: `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius: `8px`.
- Typography: `body-md` (`#0F172A`), placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus State: Border color `#009EE2`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 158, 226, 0.20)`, outline none.
- Error State: Border color `#DC2626`, focus glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(220, 38, 38, 0.15)`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Height: `28px`, padding: `0 12px`, border-radius: `rounded-full`, typography: `label-sm` uppercase.
- Variants:
  - *Active Program:* Background `#E0F2FE`, text `#005A94`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`.
  - *Innovation Highlight:* Background `rgba(0, 158, 226, 0.12)`, text `#005A94`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 158, 226, 0.3)`.
  - *Standard Neutral:* Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: `20px x 20px`, border: `1.5px solid #94A3B8`, radius: `4px` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio).
- Checked: Fill `#009EE2` with a `#002855` check/indicator, or fill `#005A94` with white. Focus ring: `3px rgba(0, 158, 226, 0.25)`.

### Curricula & Research Data Tables
- Row height: Minimum `56px`. Alternating rows transition between `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`.
- Border-bottom: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Header labels: `label-md` in `#005A94`, background `#F0F6FA`.
